## The TOJ team app (web and iPhone)

The TOJ app is for people Trail of Joy has invited to work with us. You can't sign up for it without an invite.

**What we keep about you**
- **Your account:** your name, email address and role on the team. Your password is stored only as a one-way hash.
- **What you make:** ideas, briefs, notes, the links you add, and text taken from PDFs or text files you add. Also the kit drafts you start, and approvals.
- **A team activity log:** who invited, joined, started, approved or discarded something, and when. It holds no athlete names or contact details.
- **Notifications:** if you turn them on, a token for each device you turned them on for. It's issued by your browser's push service or by Apple.

**Photos and videos**
- When you tap **Write the brief**, the photos and video stills you added go to our AI provider, Anthropic, so it can write the brief.
- TOJ doesn't store them.
- Videos are sent as a few still frames, without sound.
- File names stay on your device.

**Things you share to TOJ from other apps (iPhone)**
- They're prepared on your iPhone and stay there, in storage only the TOJ app can read, until you open TOJ.
- Opening them adds them to New idea and removes them from the phone.
- If you don't open them, they're deleted after 7 days.

**Who processes it for us**
- Render (servers) and Vercel (website).
- Supabase (database).
- Anthropic (AI that writes briefs and kits).
- Apple, and your browser's push service (notifications).

We don't sell your data, use advertising trackers, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

**Deleting your account**
- In the app, open **Team → Delete my account**.
- This deletes your sign-in, takes you off the team, deletes your pending invites and notification devices, and removes your email from the activity log and run records.
- Kits you approved stay approved, credited to "a former teammate."

**Minors**
The app follows Trail of Joy's rules on minors' data: no identifiable minor without consent on file, and a minor's data never appears on a public page by default.
