# Report Blueprint — the six-part deliverable

The exact structure of the client-facing report. Built in a Claude Design template, screen-shared on the review call, emailed after. Concrete over comprehensive — a tight report that names real tools, real steps, and a real dollar figure beats a long one full of maybes.

## Part 1 — Executive Summary
The top 3 pain points, and for each, the outcome the client gets if they implement the recommendation. Lead with the pains they feel, phrased in their words. No hype. This is the slide that has to land in 30 seconds.

## Part 2 — Priority Matrix (Effort vs Impact)
Sort every opportunity on two axes. Explicitly split into:
- **QUICK WINS** — low effort / high impact. Lead here. These are what the 4-day plan delivers.
- **MAJOR PROJECTS** — high impact / high effort. These become "what comes after."
Be honest about which is which. A knowledge system trained on a content library is high-impact but not low-effort — never disguise it as a quick win.

## Part 3 — Tool Recommendations
An in-depth review of each recommended tool:
- What it does
- The named pain it points at
- Type: basic SaaS vs custom Claude skill / GPT
- Price `[VERIFY]`
Each tool tagged with its ROI lever.

## Part 4 — Four-Day Quick-Start Plan
Four days of simple, concrete actions that land the quick wins. Day-by-day. No "explore options" — actual steps: "Day 1: connect [tool] to [app] via Zapier; test with 3 records."

## Part 5 — Financial Impact
The KPI + ROI reasoning, stated concretely: **"$X/month for Y hours/week back,"** with the math shown. Anchor on the levers. Use the owner's stated hourly value if given; otherwise use a clearly-labeled assumption and flag "confirm on review call." Example framing: *$60/mo to reclaim ~7 hrs/week.*

## Part 6 — What Comes After Quick Wins
The high-impact / high-effort projects — framed as the next engagement, not this one. This is where the implementation offer ladder gets seeded (Process Redesign, Knowledge System, Custom Workflow).

## Review-call cues (internal, not shown to client)
- Open the screen-share with the single strongest quick win.
- Close with the three questions: most urgent for you? · DIY or want help? · your timeline?

## Rules
- No `[FILL IN]` placeholders. The report ships ready.
- Never include a tool that wasn't in the Opportunity Map.
- If time/frequency numbers are missing, state assumptions explicitly rather than inventing precision.

## Cross-skill

- `roi-levers` — the tag on every opportunity and tool.
- `tool-research` — how the tools got chosen.
- `offer-ladder` — what Part 6 leads into.
