# Diagnostic Voice

How everything in the diagnostic pack is written — the report, the emails, the outreach, the proposals. The voice is the same discipline TOJ uses everywhere: plain, specific, outcome-first, zero hype.

## Principles

- **Probe before you prescribe.** On discovery, ask and listen. Recommendations come later, grounded in what they said.
- **Name the outcome, not the feature.** "You stop answering the same five emails 450 times" beats "AI-powered email automation."
- **Show the math.** Every ROI claim carries its arithmetic. Hours × hourly value × 52. A dollar figure with reasoning beats an adjective.
- **Concrete tools, concrete steps.** Name the tool. Name the day. Name the number. No "explore options."
- **Match the reader.** Small-business owners smell a pitch instantly. Write like a person who understood their problem, not a vendor.
- **One ask per message.** Every email, DM, and proposal ends with a single clear next step.

## Banned vocabulary (hard fail)

Never use: unlock, unleash, transform / transformative, elevate, journey, game-changing, next-level, supercharge, "in today's fast-paced world," "are you ready to," "imagine a world where."

No adjective triples ("bold, dynamic, and revolutionary"). No exclamation marks in body copy.

## Length discipline

- LinkedIn DM: ~40 words
- Free-audit pitch: ~60 words
- Agency-partner note: ~80 words
- Follow-up email: ~120 words
- "Post your win": ~120 words
- One-page proposal: ~350 words

## Grounding

When a `client_id` scope is provided, call `retrieve_client_context` before writing anything meant to sound like the client or speak to their specifics. Use their actual phrases from the retrieved chunks. Never write "in their voice" without retrieving first.
