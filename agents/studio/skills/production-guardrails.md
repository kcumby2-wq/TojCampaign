# Skill: Production guardrails

The lines every kit keeps, whatever the brief says. They come from the Same Work playbook, the Ground Floor Sports and Sky's the Limit agreement (Sections 8 and 9) and TOJ's house rules. If the brief or the source seems to ask you to cross one, don't: keep the rule, and name the conflict in your summary for Kyron.

## The seven rules nobody breaks (Same Work, as approved)

1. **Real words only.** Trim for length, never change meaning. No AI voices, no AI-written quotes, no composite people, no synthetic edits of any athlete.
2. **No release, no camera.** The producer confirms the consent scopes before the shoot. Texas youth athletes appear only unidentifiable (hands, cleats, backs; no face, name or jersey number) until counsel clears it.
3. **No promises.** Nothing implies an offer, a scholarship, or that the NFL is a typical result.
4. **No marks you don't own.** No team logos, NFL marks or broadcast footage, and no school logos on minors.
5. **Never alone with a minor.** No unsupervised contact, no filming in locker rooms or training rooms, and no recording a one-to-one session with a minor unless a parent is present or has consented.
6. **A pulled consent means a pulled film.** When a family withdraws, the footage comes down everywhere within 72 hours.
7. **Nothing publishes without the producer's approval,** plus Hooks's for anything under his name.

A new kit for another production carries these rules (worded for that production) on a locked card in its first column. A refresh never rewords them unless the person who started the run allowed rule changes.

## Minors

- A minor's name, face, school, team, jersey number or contact never appears in a kit. Use roles ("a 2027 receiver", "a parent") or an athlete ID.
- Texas youth stay unidentifiable: no face, name, jersey number, school or team logo, street sign or license plate.
- A parent or guardian is on set the whole time a minor is there. No locker rooms, training rooms or changing areas.
- No face-tracking or biometric tools on minor footage.
- File, project and bin names use the athlete ID, never a name.

## Honesty

- Real words, real moments. Placeholders in templates are marked as placeholders (`[Their take, verbatim]`).
- **Vintage looks never make new footage pass for old.** Year labels are always true; a camcorder date stamp shows the real date or nothing.
- Never invent an athlete result, stat, testimonial or quote, and never put words in a real person's mouth. A reference page's own line can be quoted exactly, with the page in Sources.
- No promises of offers, scholarships, placement or exposure.

## Tools that may touch footage

- **Not approved for Same Work footage:** CapCut and any ByteDance tool (no minor footage, ever); Otter.ai; cloud auto-caption or auto-clip tools until the producer reviews their terms; any generative or face-aware "enhancement" (AI upscaling that redraws faces, beauty filters, voice clones, generated B-roll, auto-reframe that crops around a face).
- No tool may train on Hooks footage, and no minor footage goes to a cloud tool whose training opt-out hasn't been checked and saved this quarter (STL agreement, Section 9).
- A tool on neither list waits for the producer: you may name it as a candidate, never as approved.

## Words

- **TOJ banned vocabulary** (never in a kit, a template, a lead, a footer or your summary): elite, generational, next-level, phenom, future star, premier, top-notch, state-of-the-art, revolutionize, unlock, unleash, transform, game-changing, supercharge. "Adobe Premiere Pro" is a product name and is fine.
- **Banned phrases:** "guaranteed exposure", "guaranteed placement".
- Don't list the banned words on a card. Say "no hype words (the TOJ list)" instead.
- Say the concrete thing: "full frame, built-in variable ND", not "cinematic powerhouse".

## Marks

- No team logos, NFL marks, broadcast footage or school logos on minors.
- The Same Work end card carries the Coached by Hooks mark only: no GFS, TOJ or Subject marks.
- Reference pages are things we learn from, not things we copy: take the approach, never their logos, layouts verbatim or footage.

## Research

- Research covers public, professional work only: published pages, official lists, reviews, product pages.
- Every outside fact goes in the Sources card with the date it was checked. Equipment approval lists and prices change: write "check the current list before buying" next to them.
- If a page won't load or sits behind a login (Instagram often does), keep what the approved kit already says, mark it "not re-checked on <date>", and say so in your summary. Never fill the gap with a guess.

## What you can't do

- You can't approve, publish or send anything. Every run makes a **draft** that Kyron reviews; only a person approves it.
- You can't make new template images. If the kit needs one, write a note card describing it for a designer, and list it in your summary.
- You can't change locked rule cards unless the run allows it. Suggest the change in your summary instead.
