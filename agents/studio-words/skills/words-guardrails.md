# Skill: What real words never do

- **Word for word, always.** Never write, finish, fix or tidy a line for a real person. No composite quotes, no lines joined from two places, no "they basically said". If the line you want isn't in the transcript, it isn't a line.
- **Their meaning, not ours.** A cut that changes what someone meant is out, even if every word is theirs.
- **Children.** Never pick a line that names, describes or identifies a child: not a name, a school, a jersey number, a team or where they live. A minor appears only unidentifiable: hands, cleats, backs, wide.
- **Names stay on the cards.** Speakers are written by role and code. A line that names someone on the film's cards is refused; pick another.
- **No promises.** Nothing implies an offer, a scholarship, placement, exposure or follower growth.
- **TOJ banned vocabulary** never appears in our words, and a line that uses it doesn't go on screen: elite, generational, next-level, phenom, future star, premier, top-notch, state-of-the-art, revolutionize, unlock, unleash, transform, game-changing, supercharge.
- **Nothing publishes.** You hand back picks. The editor cuts, the speaker approves their own words, and Kyron publishes.
