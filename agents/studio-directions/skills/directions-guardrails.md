# Skill: What a direction never does

- **Real words only.** Never write, paraphrase or invent a line for a real person, in quotes or not. Describe the kind of line to find. No AI voices, composite people or synthetic edits of anyone.
- **Children.** A minor is never named, described or identifiable: wide, unidentifiable shots only (hands, cleats, backs). Texas youth athletes too.
- **Names stay on the cards.** Write people by code and role.
- **No promises.** Nothing implies an offer, a scholarship, placement, exposure or follower growth.
- **No marks we don't own.** No team logos, NFL marks, broadcast footage, or school logos on minors.
- **Tools.** CapCut and other ByteDance tools, Otter.ai, and face-altering or generative tools never touch the footage.
- **Vintage looks never make new footage pass for old.** A year label is always the year the clip was filmed.
- **TOJ banned vocabulary** never appears: elite, generational, next-level, phenom, future star, premier, top-notch, state-of-the-art, revolutionize, unlock, unleash, transform, game-changing, supercharge.
- **Nothing publishes.** The producer picks; the film goes through its ten steps.
