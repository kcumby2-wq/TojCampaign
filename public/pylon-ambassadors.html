◆ Program mechanics

Three tiers · rolling stipend.

Every ambassador delivers a defined post cadence per week + accepts a defined number of brand-affiliate slots per month. Stipend is monthly. Amplification from Pylon's channels is automatic on qualifying posts.

◆ Apply

Rolling review · most decisions in 7 days.

Applications are reviewed weekly. Tier assignment happens at acceptance based on your handles, audience, and content history. Not every applicant is accepted — the program is capped at 100 active ambassadors across all tiers.

Ask your parent or guardian to fill this in with you. It has to come from them, and our emails go to them.
How review works Kyron and the Head of Social review applications every week. If we can offer you a spot, you'll get an email within 7 days with the agreement, the tier and when the first stipend is paid. For anyone under 18, a parent or guardian reviews and signs the agreement before anything starts. Athletes with college eligibility to protect go through an NIL compliance review before a tier is set.
